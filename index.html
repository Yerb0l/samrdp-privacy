SamRDP Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 July 2026

SamRDP (the "app") is an Android Remote Desktop (RDP) client. This policy explains how the app handles data.

Data we collect

The developer of SamRDP does not collect, store or share any personal user data. The app has no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising.

Data that stays on your device

The connection details you create (host address, username, password and settings) are stored locally on your device only. This data is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Network connections

When you connect to a remote computer, the app opens a direct RDP connection between your device and the computer or server you specify. Credentials are sent only to the host you connect to and never pass through any developer servers.

Permissions

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — to reach your computer over the network.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, WAKE_LOCK — to keep an active session alive while the app is in the background and to show a notification with a disconnect action.
  • File access (Android 12 and below) — to exchange files between the device and the remote desktop when you ask it to.

The app does not use the microphone, camera, contacts or location data.

Children

The app is a productivity tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
